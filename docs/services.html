SEO & AEO, explained

Help customers find
clear answers.

SEO helps search engines understand your website and helps people find relevant information. AEO focuses on making your content useful for answer-based search: direct answers, well-organised pages and accurate business details.

My approach connects content, search and the wider digital presence. We start with your website and customer questions, identify practical improvements, and agree on the work to implement.

Does AEO guarantee a place in AI answers? +

No. Google says the same SEO fundamentals apply to its AI search features; no special AI markup is required. Search engines and AI systems decide what to show. Rankings, citations and leads cannot be guaranteed.
