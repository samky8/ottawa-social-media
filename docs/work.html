The Sunday Table

Restaurant · Feed concept

A fictional restaurant concept: food-led art direction and a short invitation to book a table. Created using stock photography.

Inside the creative direction +

The idea

Make the dining experience tangible before someone books.

The creative choices

Warm photography, generous type and one simple invitation keep the attention on the meal.

Daybreak Coffee

Café · Reel cover concept

A fictional café concept: a vertical Reel cover and a repeatable visual direction for everyday rituals. A static concept, not a filmed Reel.

Inside the creative direction +

The idea

Turn a familiar morning ritual into a recognisable café story.

The creative choices

A portrait crop, a short headline and a consistent name treatment give a Reel cover a clear point of view.

Scope: concept layout, copy and art direction. Illustrative photography; no performance claims.

Gather Kitchen

Catering · Feed concept

A fictional catering concept: behind-the-scenes imagery and copy that puts the preparation front and centre. No client relationship or results are implied.

The idea

The creative choices
