The practical details

Social media
package FAQs

Do you film at our location?+

Yes. Each package includes on-site content shoots. We plan around your space, offerings and day-to-day operations. Travel within the National Capital Region is included. We serve clients within the NCR.

What do we need to provide?+

Share your priorities, upcoming promotions, brand materials and appropriate account access. Have the products, space or people being featured ready for the shoot. We will agree who reviews content and how feedback is shared.

Which platforms are included?+

Instagram and Facebook are included in the monthly packages. TikTok and Stories are not part of the listed deliverables and can be discussed separately.

Can we approve content?+

You approve content before publishing. Each monthly content batch includes one consolidated revision round. Additional changes and reshoots are quoted separately.

How are posts counted?+

Each package lists monthly totals. Reels and additional feed posts are separate unique pieces; adapting one piece for both Instagram and Facebook does not count as two. Higher tiers do not add their quantities to the previous tier.

Can you use existing photos?+

Yes, suitable photos and videos you have permission to use can help tell the story between shoots. We will review their quality, fit and usage rights.

What does reporting include?+

Essential includes a monthly performance summary. Growth includes analytics and recommendations. Premium includes a monthly strategy meeting. Reporting helps us discuss what resonated and what to try next; it is not a promise of sales or leads.

Are ads included?+

No. Paid advertising spend and advertising management are separate from these packages. Extra filming and other additional work are quoted separately.

What is the commitment?+

Packages start with a three-month initial commitment. The founder’s offer takes 15% off your first two months; regular monthly pricing applies from month three.

What areas do you serve?+

We work with businesses in Ottawa and throughout the National Capital Region. Travel within the NCR is included in your package.
